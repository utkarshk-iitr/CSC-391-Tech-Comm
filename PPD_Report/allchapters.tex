\chapter*{Competition - IIT Pokerbots 2026}

Participated in IIT Pokerbots 2026, a pan-IIT algorithmic competition focused on designing autonomous agents for decision-making under uncertainty. The task involved developing a poker-playing bot for a custom variant of heads-up poker known as \textit{Sneak Peek Hold'em}, where players operate with incomplete information and must optimize betting strategies using probabilistic reasoning and game theory.

Unlike standard Texas Hold'em, this variant introduces an additional auction phase after the flop, allowing players to bid for partial information about the opponent’s hand, thereby increasing the strategic complexity of the game. Each match consists of multiple automated rounds where bots compete to maximize cumulative chip earnings.

The competition was conducted in two stages:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Online Prelims (28 Feb -- 7 Mar 2026):} Bots competed in a round-robin format against other submissions.
    \item \textbf{Offline Finals (28 -- 29 Mar 2026, IIT Madras):} Top teams were shortlisted for final matches, presentations, and evaluation.
\end{itemize}

Achieved \textbf{Rank 35} in the competition, demonstrating strong performance and a robust algorithmic approach incorporating strategic decision-making, opponent modeling, and expected value optimization.
